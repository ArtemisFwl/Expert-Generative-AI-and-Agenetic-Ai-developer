The most famous speech by Jawaharlal Nehru was delivered on the midnight of 14–15 August 1947, when India became independent from the British Empire.
It is called the “Tryst with Destiny” speech and was delivered in the Parliament House during the historic moment of the Indian Independence.

Famous Opening Lines

“Long years ago we made a tryst with destiny, and now the time comes when we shall redeem our pledge — not wholly or in full measure, but very substantially.”

Most Famous Part

“At the stroke of the midnight hour, when the world sleeps, India will awake to life and freedom.”

Short Excerpt from the Speech

“A moment comes, which comes but rarely in history, when we step out from the old to the new, when an age ends, and when the soul of a nation, long suppressed, finds utterance.”

Meaning of the Speech (Simple)

Nehru was saying that:

India had waited many years for freedom.

At midnight, India finally became a free nation.

The responsibility now was to build a strong, democratic and just country.

Freedom was not the end; it was the beginning of a new journey.

Why This Speech Is Important

It marks the birth of modern India.

It is one of the most famous political speeches in world history.

It reflects the vision of Jawaharlal Nehru for a united, democratic, and progressive India.
